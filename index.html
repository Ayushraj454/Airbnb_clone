Wake up to soft morning light pouring through eight-foot cast-iron windows in this converted 1920s garment-factory loft. Exposed brick, white-oak floors, and a curated mix of mid-century furniture set the tone for a stay that feels equal parts gallery and home.

The open living area flows into a chef's kitchen stocked with everything you need for a home-cooked meal, while the primary bedroom offers a king bed and a reading nook overlooking Greene Street. A second bedroom with two twin beds is perfect for kids or a second couple.

Steps from Broadway's flagship boutiques, five-star restaurants, and the 6/N/R/W subway lines, you're never more than a ten-minute walk from anywhere downtown Manhattan has to offer. Washington Square Park and the West Village are a short stroll south.

Building has a virtual doorman and package room. Self check-in with a smart lock means no waiting around — you can arrive any time after 3pm.
